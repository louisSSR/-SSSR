# 白角色插画

两幅图均使用内置 image_gen 工具生成并复制进本项目，作为本地商店的白角色主题素材。大幅主视觉和 Q 版小助手同时保留；不是官方素材包。运行时不请求外部图片服务。

## 主视觉提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: portrait illustration for a Chinese SillyTavern butterfly-causality shop UI themed on Shiro from No Game No Life. Create an original polished anime fan illustration of Shiro, fully clothed, age-appropriate, thoughtful and calm, long flowing white hair fading through icy blue, lavender and pale pink, amber-red eyes, tiny golden crown tilted atop her head, oversized purple sailor uniform with long sleeves, modest chest-up portrait. She holds a small white chess queen, a delicate glowing cyan butterfly near it. Refined thin plum linework, airy pastel watercolor highlights, beautifully rendered hair, recognizable character, no text or watermark, no sexualization. Portrait composition with head entirely visible, enough breathing room, transparent background and clean alpha edges. Tall 2:3 composition. Intended to sit on the right side of an ice-white/pale-violet app header.

## Q 版提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new separate cute chibi mascot asset of Shiro from No Game No Life for the same butterfly shop. Full body, super-deformed two-heads-tall chibi, oversized round head, tiny short body and hands, long fluffy white hair with pastel ice-blue, lavender and pink gradient, large calm amber-red eyes, tiny gold crown, fully clothed in a modest oversized purple sailor school uniform with long sleeves and opaque dark leggings, no skin exposure except face/hands, no sexualization. Sitting adorably next to a white chess queen almost as tall as her tiny body, a little glowing cyan butterfly beside her head. Gentle curious expression, soft clean anime linework and polished pastel shading, recognizable Shiro, clean silhouette for a tiny app mascot. Transparent background and clean alpha edges, full crown and hair and feet entirely visible with generous margins. Square image, no text, no watermark. This is a small Q版白 sidekick and must be clearly chibi, not normal proportions.
